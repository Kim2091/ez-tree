The opaque maps in `foliage/` are generated by `scripts/bake_surfaces.py`. The
aspen bark set combines the grain of ambientCG Bark007 with procedural lenticels.

`PhotoSmooth_1K-JPG` and `PhotoFurrowed_1K-JPG` are direct color extractions from user-supplied photographs P1250194_DxO and P1250234_DxO, respectively. Their species are not confirmed; they are not ambientCG assets. They contain color maps only.
