# Northern Illinois photo reference notes

The photographs stay in the user's local Pictures folder; they are not included in this repository. Photo numbers below refer to the original `P125xxxx_DxO.jpg` files. Capture sequence is a clue, not proof that a fallen leaf came from the nearest trunk.

- **0184, 0188:** bare-crown views of young street trees. Species is not identifiable confidently from crown shape alone.
- **0186:** fallen, toothed, three-major-lobed maple-type leaf. Red maple or a red-maple hybrid is plausible; the image alone does not establish a species. Compare [red maple](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/red-maple/) and [Freeman maple](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/freemans-maple/).
- **0187, 0231, 0242:** mixed or fallen foliage/ground context. Individual leaves cannot safely be tied to a photographed trunk from the image alone.
- **0189, 0190, 0198, 0199, 0201, 0210, 0222:** whole-tree views useful for silhouette and branching; species unconfirmed.
- **0191, 0192:** fallen five-lobed maple leaves. Sugar and Norway maple are possibilities, but a detached fall leaf is insufficient for a confident species ID. Compare [sugar maple](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/sugar-maple/0/) and [Norway maple](https://extension.umn.edu/natural-resources/forestry-and-wildlife/invasive-species/norway-maple).
- **0193-0197:** smooth to lightly fissured bark and root flare, likely a single photographed tree based on sequence; species unconfirmed. 0194 is the input for the optional Photo Smooth color candidate.
- **0213, 0215, 0217-0220:** smooth bark, exposed base, and branch junctions, likely a single tree based on sequence. Maple-like foliage appears in the canopy; species unconfirmed.
- **0228-0229:** bark and upper crown, species unconfirmed.
- **0234-0238, 0240-0241:** furrowed bark, branch forks, root base, and a wound, likely a single tree based on sequence; species unconfirmed. 0234 is the input for the optional Photo Furrowed color candidate.

The two project texture candidates are AI-generated interpretations of the indicated photos, not calibrated scans. They have no measured normal, roughness, or height data and may show seams when repeated. They remain optional and are not assigned to a species preset.
